The £59-with-release scenario produced the strongest MRR outcome in this model, but it cannot answer your actual question yet: the **under-4% monthly churn limit was not assessed**. Monthly churn is modelled as a calculated effect, while the 4% limit is a level; without a compatible observed churn baseline/frame, the comparison cannot test whether any option stays below it.

The result is otherwise robust under the tested assumptions, but it rests materially on the assumed churn rate, subscriber base, and perceived value of the release. It also assumes that *more* MRR is the goal direction.
