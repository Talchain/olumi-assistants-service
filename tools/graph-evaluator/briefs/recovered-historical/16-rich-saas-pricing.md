We run a B2B SaaS platform with 1,200 active accounts and £720k ARR. Our goal is to maximise MRR over the next 12 months. We're considering three pricing options: raise the standard plan from £49/month to £59/month, keep it at £49/month, or introduce a tiered pricing model with Basic (£39), Standard (£49), and Premium (£79). Key factors we're tracking are churn rate (currently 4.2% monthly), conversion rate from trial (18%), and average deal size (£490 annually). We have a hard constraint of £50k marketing spend cap for any pricing transition campaign. Our main competitors charge between £45 and £65 per month.
